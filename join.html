Interview Information

Interview Structure

Attempt each question, explain your reasoning clearly, think independently and maintain integrity.

Behavioral Interview

We assess communication skills, professionalism, independence, and motivation.

Technical (2 Questions)

A foundational quantitative question designed to be relatively quick and a more complex quantitative problem focused on structure, logic, and assumptions.

Expected value · basic probability · logic puzzles · exponential growth · intro finance
LeetCode-Style Coding

You may be asked to code live, explain complexity, and discuss edge cases.

Array/list manipulation · sorting and merging · parentheses validation · number transformations · basic algorithms
How We Evaluate
Behavioral fit Foundational technical Advanced technical Coding ability

Candidates are compared within similar academic levels.

We look for members who will contribute meaningfully, grow technically, strengthen the FQE network, and take projects seriously.

Note on Integrity

Technical interviews assess independent reasoning ability.

  • Calculators are not allowed
  • AI tools are not allowed
  • You may be asked to show written work and/or share your screen during coding

Individuals found cheating during interviews will be disqualified from consideration.
