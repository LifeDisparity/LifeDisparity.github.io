About

FQE @
Baruch

Financial Quants and Engineers (FQE) develops technical depth and professional readiness at the intersection of quantitative finance and technology. Members work across data science, risk analysis, quantitative modeling, and algorithmic trading through workshops, technical discussions, and practical projects. Our goal is to help Baruch undergraduates build the skills, portfolio, and network needed for competitive technical pathways in quant and beyond.

01WorkshopsApplied sessions in computer science, math, hackathons, and computer architecture.
02ProjectsEnd-to-end research, backtests, and real-world applications.See our projects →
03MentorshipDirect access to alumni, faculty, and industry leaders shaping the future of finance.
