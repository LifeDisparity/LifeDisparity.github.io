Competition Structure

Trading Workflow

  1. Step 0 · Due Sep 28Interest & ApplicationSubmit the interest form by the application deadline to be considered. Applications are open to current CUNY students.
  2. Step 1 · Before trading dayCompetition BriefingParticipants receive instructions and logistics before the live session. Final location details are shared once confirmed.
  3. Step 2 · 1:00 to 3:15pmSession 1Trade in the first live competition session. Apply discretionary or systematic strategies in real time.
  4. Step 3 · 3:15 to 3:45pmBreak and RefinementUse the 30-minute break to review performance and refine the strategy. Carry insights from Session 1 into Session 2.
  5. Step 4 · 3:45 to 6:00pmSession 2Trade in the final live session. Final rankings are determined after the session concludes.

Competition Structure

Semester Workflow

  1. Phase 0 · KickoffTesting and Validation
    • A GitHub Classroom environment will be provided.
    • Teams complete a small coding problem for validation.
  2. Phase 1 · 2 to 3 weeksProposal
    • Submit a 2 to 3 page proposal (preferably LaTeX).
    • Include problem statement, literature review, framework, methodology, and roadmap/data sources.
    • Judged on originality, mathematical depth, feasibility, and clarity.
    • Based on interest and funding, teams may be split into tracks such as quantitative finance, general applied mathematics, or AI and learning systems.
  3. Phase 2 · 6 to 10 weeksResearch and Implementation
    • Produce formal documentation/paper and code repository.
    • Provide reproducible results with experimental validation and tests.
    • Use proper notation, explicit assumptions, and statistical testing where applicable.
  4. Phase 3 · 20 minutesFinal Presentation
    • 12 minutes for research presentation.
    • 8 minutes for technical Q&A.
    • Judging panel includes professors, researchers, alumni, and practitioners.
